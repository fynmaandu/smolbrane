Fock Representation of Massive Field Algebra

The vacuum state on the extended asymptotic Klein-Gordon algebra 𝒜in,QPKG is the Gaussian algebraic state ω0KG with vanishing 1-point function and 2-point function given by $$\begin{equation} \label{Vacuums_KG} \omega^{\text{KG}}_0\big( \boldsymbol{b}(w_1)\boldsymbol{b}(w_2)^* \big) = \frac{4(2\pi)^3}{m^2} \langle w_1, w_2\rangle_{\mathcal{H}}\hspace{1em} \omega^{\text{KG}}_0\big( c(w_1)c(w_2)^* \big) = \frac{4(2\pi)^3}{m^2} \langle w_2, w_1\rangle_{\mathcal{H}} \end{equation}$$ for all test functions w1(p), w2(p), where ⟨,⟩ℋ is the L2 inner product on ℋ−. Furthermore, ω0KG is an eigenstate of eigenvalue zero of 𝒬i−(λ) and all of the Poincaré generators.

GNS Construction (Massive Scalar Field)

The GNS construction for ω0KG yields a Hilbert space ℱKG with a natural Fock space structure.

  • Concretely this construction is obtained as follows.

  • On the space of complex test functions on ℋ− we define the inner product

$$\begin{equation} \langle w_1 | w_2 \rangle := \omega_0^{\mathrm{KG}}\big(\mathbf{b}(w_1)\mathbf{b}(w_2)^*\big) = \frac{4(2\pi)^3}{m^2} \langle w_1, w_2 \rangle_{\mathcal{H}} \end{equation}$$

using the 2-point function in [Vacuums_KG] .

  • This is a $\underbrace{\text{non-degenerate}}$, positive, Hermitian inner product

i.e., ℋKG is the Hilbert space of square-integrable functions (i.e. wave packets) of the timelike momentum p represented as points on ℋ−.

  • This Hilbert space serves as the “one particle” Hilbert space for particles in the Fock space.

The Fock space of particles is given by

$$\begin{equation} \mathscr{F}^{\mathrm{KG}}_{\mathrm{particles}} = \mathbb{C} \oplus \bigoplus_{n \geq 1} \underbrace{ \mathcal{H}^{\mathrm{KG}}_S \otimes \cdots \otimes_S \mathcal{H}^{\mathrm{KG}} }_{n~\text{times}} \end{equation}$$

where ⊗S is the symmetrized tensor product. On this Fock space the b(w)* acts as a creation operator and b(w) acts as the annihilation operator for a particle wave packet w(p).
